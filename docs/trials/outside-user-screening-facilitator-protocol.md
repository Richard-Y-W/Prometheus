# Outside-user project-folder screening protocol

## Eligibility and setup

Use one adult participant who is not the project owner, did not implement
Prometheus, is not an AI agent, and has not rehearsed this task. A colleague or
engineer may participate; prior CAD experience is recorded but is not required.

Give the participant only the verified ZIP, the participant task sheet, and
ordinary access to the test Windows account. Do not give them source code,
expected counts, prior scan output, this protocol, or a completed form. Record
the ZIP hash and verify its embedded manifest before the participant begins.
Obtain consent for notes and separate consent for any screen recording.

## Unassisted portion

Start timing when the participant begins the first task. Ask them to think
aloud, but do not point to controls, name a filter, interpret a status, select a
file, or confirm an answer. Neutral prompts such as “What are you looking for?”
are allowed only to resume thinking aloud.

If the participant asks for help, record the exact question first. Assistance
needed to proceed ends the unassisted portion unless it only repairs an
external machine or package failure. Record every intervention, its time, and
what changed. Do not silently reset the application or edit the project.

## Observation standard

Record visible actions, timestamps, exact statements, errors, confusion, and
attempted workarounds. Use `not observed` for missing evidence. Do not convert
hesitation into understanding, summarize an incorrect statement as correct, or
omit a mistaken claim that the project passed.

After the unassisted portion, questions may be asked to clarify what the
participant meant, but label those answers as post-task. Separate product
friction from packaging or machine failures. Redact personal identifiers only,
and identify each redaction.

## Evidence boundary

The session exists only after a real eligible participant completes it and a
factual record is saved. A developer rehearsal, automated test, AI review, or
blank form cannot satisfy Program 01D. Participant success is not required;
honest observation is the gate.
