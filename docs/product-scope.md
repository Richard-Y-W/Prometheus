# V1 product scope

Prometheus targets small robotics, benchtop electromechanical machines, laboratory equipment, and prototype automation fixtures. The north-star workflow is: import an assembly, bind sourced components, confirm interfaces and joints, define a scenario, and receive reproducible pass/warning/fail/indeterminate findings.

V1 owns component/evidence models, semantic assembly, scenario planning, cheap native checks, solver adapters, provenance, uncertainty, and findings. It does not claim certification or implement universal rigid-body dynamics, nonlinear FEA, CFD, electromagnetic simulation, or cinematic simulation playback.

The first complete native slice is a multi-part STEP motor arm with a reviewed motor revision, confirmed frame/joint, rotate/lift scenario, collision/CoG/motor/current/battery checks, save/reopen, and offline reproduction.
